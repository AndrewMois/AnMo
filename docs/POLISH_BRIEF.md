# Post-launch polish brief

**Status:** analysis only. No visual or production changes are authorized by this document.

This brief distils Andriy's first live-site review on 2026-08-15. It is the source of truth for the next revision round and should be updated as further spoken feedback arrives.

## The central diagnosis

The site is visually precise and technically strong, but its restraint has crossed into emotional distance. It currently introduces a polished quality-engineering system before it introduces Andriy.

The problem is not simply “too much text.” Three things compound one another:

1. **The page asks for too much reading before attachment.** Long paragraphs, repeated labels, and five-part evidence lists make each case feel like documentation.
2. **The voice is consistently formal.** Abstract phrases such as “clear engineering evidence,” “systems understood in depth,” and “tools in service of judgement” are credible in isolation but accumulate into an institutional, AI-written tone.
3. **Andriy arrives too late.** The portrait appears near the bottom, so the visitor meets a profession and a methodology long before meeting the person.

The revision should not make the site louder or more sales-driven. It should make it more human, faster to understand, more factual, and more memorable.

## Desired shift

| Current impression                             | Revised impression                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A beautifully typeset technical report         | A sharp personal presentation with technical depth behind it      |
| Quiet authority                                | Human authority with wit and nerve                                |
| Method first                                   | Person and value first                                            |
| Abstract claims supported by long explanations | Facts, scale, and specific ownership supported by optional detail |
| Repeated case-study template                   | Distinct visual episodes with different rhythms                   |
| Portrait as a late pause                       | Immediate human connection in the hero                            |
| Development as a separate explanation          | Development as an obvious reason the QA work is deeper            |

The emotional target is: **alive, powerful, slightly irreverent, and expensive in craft — never arrogant, needy, or corporate.**

## What must remain

- The public title remains `QA Automation Engineer`.
- The site stays fast, accessible, responsive, and structurally clean.
- The inspection spine and its cobalt signal remain.
- Cream, graphite, gold, and cobalt remain the core family.
- The line `I know where systems break because I know how they are built.` is a strong idea worth preserving or tightening.
- BluWave AI, Canada Revenue Agency, and Young Mystic remain the three primary evidence stories.
- Claims remain truthful and confidentiality-safe. Personality does not grant permission to invent outcomes or metrics.

## What should change

### 1. Put Andriy in the hero

Use the supplied portrait immediately, ideally as an asymmetric cutout or irregular photographic form that interrupts the grid. Avoid a circular avatar, conventional profile card, or decorative blob.

The hero should establish four things within seconds:

- Andriy is a QA Automation Engineer;
- he is a real, approachable person;
- he can build the systems he tests;
- his confidence has a point of view.

`with a developer's perspective` should be replaced. It is accurate but sounds like positioning language rather than something Andriy would say.

The coordinate detail should become a personal micro-line or easter egg. It should reward attention rather than repeat geography. Working territory, not approved copy:

- `Yes, I tested this site.`
- `Ship it? Let me look first.`
- `Built. Broken. Better.`
- a more personal line discovered during the copy workshop.

### 2. Move the practical range near the top

The current `Working range / Tools in service of judgement` section is important evidence and is buried too late. Move a condensed form immediately after the hero, or integrate it into the hero/proof transition.

Rename it in plain English. The visitor should instantly understand “this is what he actually works with.” Avoid a wall of badges and avoid proficiency meters.

Possible structures:

- five compact territories with one concrete tool line each;
- one horizontal “what I cover” field;
- three grouped systems: interfaces, services/data, delivery/performance/ML.

### 3. Shorten and differentiate the cases

The current repeated sequence — `Context`, `Response`, `Demonstrates` — is readable but feels like an audit report. Replace it with a presentation hierarchy that can be scanned before it is read.

Each primary case should contain:

- one human headline;
- one sentence that establishes scale or responsibility;
- three strong proof points maximum;
- a compact technology/evidence line;
- optional detail only when it adds something new.

Move the most persuasive evidence upward. `Demonstrates` should not be a concluding taxonomy; it can become an opening proof strip or disappear when the headline and facts already communicate it.

Target a substantial reduction in visible copy, approximately one third to one half across the whole page. Reduction must come from removing repetition and abstractions, not from deleting the evidence that makes the work credible.

### 4. Give each case its own visual memory

The cases should remain part of one system but stop feeling like the same template repeated three times.

#### BluWave AI

- Primary professional story and strongest proof of scope.
- The subtext should communicate that Andriy is one of two QA engineers carrying quality across a startup-sized product surface, without using empty superlatives.
- Present framework ownership as the result: mobile/UI, API, SOAP, performance, CI evidence, and ML validation were not isolated test scripts but quality infrastructure built for the team.
- Explore a restrained energy/forecasting atmosphere: a faint time-series trace, grid-load rhythm, data ribbon, renewable-energy light, or company-adjacent color cue.
- An official public logo may be evaluated as a small identifier, not as the visual concept and never in a way that implies endorsement.

#### Canada Revenue Agency

- More compact than BluWave AI.
- A subtle Canadian signifier may help immediate recognition: restrained red, a small maple-leaf/flag cue, or editorial government-document geometry.
- Avoid making the section look like an official CRA page.
- Lead with the reusable negative-testing framework and the trust/responsibility of federal delivery.

#### Young Mystic

- Present as a live product, not “the product behind the tester.”
- Lead with `200+ users`, long-term ownership, and the fact that Andriy built the full system.
- A real product image or live-site visual is more valuable than another paragraph.
- This case is the clearest proof of `I know how systems break because I build them.`

### 5. Make the English sound spoken, not generated

The new voice should use:

- shorter sentences;
- contractions where natural;
- concrete verbs (`built`, `rewrote`, `traced`, `shipped`, `broke`, `fixed`);
- first-person ownership;
- specific facts before interpretation;
- occasional dry humour or challenge;
- one bold statement at a time.

Avoid accumulations of:

- abstract nouns (`judgement`, `evidence`, `behaviour`, `capability`, `structure`);
- ceremonious phrases (`systems understood in depth`, `quality begins before…`);
- consultant language;
- repeated claims that the visitor should infer from the work;
- sentences that Andriy would never naturally say aloud.

The test for every line: **Would Andriy plausibly say this to a strong engineer over coffee?** If not, rewrite it.

Bold statements are welcome when they carry personality or a fact. Working territory, not approved final copy:

- `QA done properly.`
- `I test the part behind the button.`
- `I build test systems from scratch — because sometimes the existing one is the bug.`
- `I know where systems break. I build them too.`

### 6. Add restrained life to the visual system

The current steel-blue phrase in the hero feels emotionally grey. Re-evaluate it after the new portrait and headline are composed; color should follow the composition rather than be changed in isolation.

Explore restrained living material:

- burnished gold catching light at selected edges;
- rare cobalt-to-gold interference or energy traces;
- a subtle grain, shimmer, or wave that appears during scroll;
- one or two surprising breaks in the square grid;
- depth created through photography and light rather than more cards.

Do not introduce generic glitter, constant ambient animation, loud gold gradients, or black-and-gold luxury clichés. “Luxury” here means material care and a memorable gesture.

### 7. Use live products, not source-code calls to action

Public project CTAs should open the finished work. Do not show `View source` for Young Mystic, Guided by Scent, or Alex Healing. GitHub may remain as a restrained profile link in the footer, but it is not the primary proof for these products.

Confirmed live destinations:

- Young Mystic: `https://young-mystic.com`
- Guided by Scent: `https://anna-home-five.vercel.app`
- Alex Healing: `https://alexhealing.com`

## Proposed page architecture

This is a structural direction, not an approved wireframe.

1. **Hero with portrait** — role, sharper human line, short summary, primary CTA, easter egg.
2. **What I actually work across** — condensed practical capability range.
3. **One strong point of view** — likely a tightened version of `I know where systems break because I know how they are built.`
4. **BluWave AI** — the deepest and most visually distinctive professional story.
5. **Canada Revenue Agency** — compact, factual, immediately recognizable.
6. **Products I built** — Young Mystic first, Guided by Scent and Alex Healing as live visual builds.
7. **Short personal close** — curiosity, pharmacy background, and human responsibility without repeating the hero portrait treatment.
8. **Contact** — direct and minimal.

The separate four-step “quality approach” section is a candidate for removal or compression. Its strongest idea can live inside the cases; the current version explains how a QA professional thinks before the visitor has decided to care about this particular QA professional.

## Copy and design workflow before implementation

### Stage A — Content cut

- Mark every current line as `keep`, `rewrite`, `merge`, or `remove`.
- Produce a shorter text-only page outline.
- Draft three hero directions with different levels of wit and provocation.
- Draft the BluWave AI case first as the voice benchmark.

**Approval gate:** Andriy chooses the voice and confirms the factual emphasis before layout changes.

### Stage B — Structural prototype

- Create a low-fidelity content wireframe for desktop and mobile.
- Place the capability range near the top.
- Test one shortened professional case and one live product case.
- Confirm the target page length and what detail is intentionally omitted.

**Approval gate:** the page can be understood by scanning headings, proof points, and imagery without reading every paragraph.

### Stage C — Visual prototype

- Build only the new hero and one BluWave AI episode.
- Derive a new portrait treatment without altering the approved source.
- Explore one restrained gold/cobalt living-material effect.
- Evaluate a BluWave AI identifier or abstract energy/forecast visual using public brand context.

**Approval gate:** the result feels more like Andriy within the first screen and does not become a recruiter template, employer imitation, or decorative luxury page.

### Stage D — Full implementation

- Apply the approved voice and structure to the remaining sections.
- Replace source links with verified live links.
- Remove redundant late-page content and any duplicate portrait role.
- Preserve accessibility, reduced motion, performance budgets, and no-JavaScript reading.
- Run local and hosted automated checks before a production push.

## Decisions still needed

- Final hero line and how provocative it should be.
- Final easter egg.
- Whether the hero portrait is a photographic crop, a clean cutout, or a hybrid material treatment.
- Whether BluWave AI uses the official public logo as a small identifier or only an original energy/data motif.
- Whether the four-step quality approach disappears entirely or survives as a very short point-of-view fragment.
- Which live screenshots best represent Young Mystic, Guided by Scent, and Alex Healing.

Until these are approved, the next action is continued analysis and copy exploration, not implementation.
