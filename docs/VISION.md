# Vision

## What this site is

AnMo is Andriy's professional digital home: a single-page portrait of a quality engineer who understands products from interface to infrastructure because he also builds complete systems himself.

The site is not an online CV and not a catalogue of technologies. It should leave a visitor with one clear feeling:

> This person sees farther into a system than its visible surface. His judgment is calm, his work is deliberate, and quality is safe in his hands.

## Positioning

Primary identity: **QA Automation Engineer with a developer's perspective.**

Andriy moves comfortably between strategy and hands-on engineering. He can understand an unfamiliar system, determine where meaningful risk lives, build the test architecture it needs, trace failures across layers, and communicate the evidence clearly.

Development is not a competing identity. It is the foundation of his QA depth. He understands how interfaces, APIs, services, data, authentication, devices, CI/CD, and deployment decisions influence one another because he has designed and shipped products across those layers.

## Desired presence

The portfolio should feel confident without sounding needy or theatrical. It does not shout that Andriy can do everything. It selects a few difficult things, presents them with exceptional clarity, and trusts the visitor to recognize the level.

The desired energy is:

- personal rather than corporate;
- assured rather than sales-driven;
- luxurious through restraint and workmanship rather than decorative excess;
- warm without becoming wellness branding;
- technical without cyberpunk, terminal, or dashboard clichés;
- creative without sacrificing recruiter speed or accessibility.

## The central metaphor

**Seeing the system beneath the surface.**

A visual signal passes through layers, reveals relationships, meets uncertainty, and resolves into clarity. The same metaphor shapes the narrative: understand the system, locate risk, apply pressure, automate evidence, and make a decision.

## Success criteria

Within ten seconds, a visitor understands Andriy's role and value. Within two minutes, they see credible evidence of quality-engineering depth. After the full page, they remember a distinct person rather than another candidate template.

The site succeeds when it feels equally credible to a recruiter, hiring manager, QA lead, software engineer, and product-minded founder.
